## Your goal

Put the four white edges around the white center. Each edge's other sticker must match the center on its side. Ignore corners for now.

## Make a daisy

Hold yellow on top. Bring the four white edge stickers around the yellow center, like petals. A white edge in the middle layer can usually move up with a quarter turn of its side face. A white edge underneath can move up with a half turn.

Before lifting an edge, turn U to leave an empty petal position above it. If a white sticker faces sideways, move that edge into the middle layer, then lift it onto the top with a side-face turn. Work on one edge at a time; the daisy doesn't need matching side colors yet.

## Send each petal home

1. Keep yellow on top. Turn U until a petal's side sticker matches the center directly below it.
2. Turn that matching side face halfway. The white sticker moves underneath.
3. Repeat for the other three petals. Use U to line them up; don't turn the finished bottom layer.

## Check before continuing

Look underneath for a white cross. Look around the sides for four matching edge-center pairs. A white edge beside the wrong center is not finished: lift it back into the daisy and realign it.
