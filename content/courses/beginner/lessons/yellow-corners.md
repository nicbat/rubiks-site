## Your goal

Put each yellow corner between its three matching centers. It may still be twisted; the yellow sticker doesn't need to face up yet.

## Find a corner in the right place

Inspect all three stickers on each top corner. A yellow-green-orange corner belongs between the yellow, green, and orange centers, whatever direction its stickers face.

If you find a correctly placed corner, rotate the whole cube so it sits at the **front-right-top**. Keep it there during the sequence. If none is correctly placed, do the sequence once from any front side, then look again.

## Move the other three corners

The sequence cycles the other three corners, keeping the front-right corner in place. Do it once and inspect. If necessary, repeat it once more with the same front-right corner.

## Check before continuing

Every corner must sit between its matching centers. The yellow edges should remain aligned, and the lower layers should remain solved. Only corner twists are left.
