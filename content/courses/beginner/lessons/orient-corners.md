## Your goal

Turn the remaining yellow corners so that yellow faces up. This is the final step. Read the whole lesson before starting.

## Keep one grip

Hold yellow on top and an unsolved corner at the **front-right-top**. Remember the front center color. From now until all corners are oriented, **do not rotate the whole cube**.

Use the four-move group `R' D' R D`. Repeat that complete group twice or four times until the corner's yellow sticker faces up. Always finish the final D move, even if the yellow sticker looks correct one move earlier.

## Bring the next corner to the same spot

Turn **only U** to bring another twisted corner to the front-right-top. Keep the front center facing you. Repeat complete four-move groups until this corner faces up too.

The lower layers temporarily look broken. Continue with the same grip; do not try to repair them between corners. They return when you finish every corner correctly.

The example handles two twisted corners: two groups for the first, U to the next, four groups for the second, then U' to align the finished layer. Your cube may need different U turns or a different number of groups.

## Finish and check

Once every yellow corner faces up, turn U to align the top layer with the centers. All six faces should now be solved.

If the lower layers remain mixed, check that you kept the same front center and completed every four-move group. Reset the example and step through it before trying again. Don't turn an individual corner by hand.
