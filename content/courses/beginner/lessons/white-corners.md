## Your goal

Finish the white layer, including the matching band of colors around its sides. Keep white underneath.

## Find a corner's home

Choose a white corner in the top layer. Its other two colors tell you which two side centers it belongs between. Turn the whole cube so that this slot is at the **front-right**. Turn U to put the chosen corner directly above that slot.

## Insert the corner

Do the four-move right-hand sequence shown below. Repeat the entire sequence until that corner is correctly inserted. Depending on how the white sticker faces, it can take several repetitions. Keep the same corner and slot in view; don't turn U separately between repetitions.

Then choose another white corner. You may rotate the whole cube to bring its empty slot to the front-right before starting the next insertion.

## If a corner is stuck underneath

If it is in the wrong slot or twisted, hold it at the front-right and do the sequence once to lift it into the top layer. Find its correct home and insert it from there.

## Check before continuing

The whole white face should be complete. The bottom row on every side must match that side's center. Leave the white layer underneath for the rest of the course.
