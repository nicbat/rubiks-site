## Your goal

Make four yellow edge stickers face upward. Ignore the yellow corners and side alignment for this step.

## Find your pattern

Keep yellow on top. Count only the four edge positions around the yellow center.

- **Cross:** all four face upward. Go to the next lesson.
- **Line:** hold the two upward yellow edges left-to-right.
- **L shape:** hold the upward edges at the back and left.
- **Dot:** none face upward. Start from any front side.

## Make the cross

For a line, do the sequence below once. For an L, do it once, then inspect and hold the resulting line left-to-right before repeating. For a dot, do it once, inspect the L, and follow the same instructions. Recheck your pattern between complete sequences.

## Check before continuing

All four top edges must show yellow. The first two layers should still be complete. If only one or three yellow edges face up with both lower layers solved, recheck the cube; that is not a reachable pattern on a normally assembled 3×3.
