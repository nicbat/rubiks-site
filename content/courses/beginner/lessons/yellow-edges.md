## Your goal

Keep the yellow cross and match all four top edges to their side centers. Ignore corners for now.

## Find two matches

Turn U and look around the sides. If all four edges match their centers, this step is finished. Otherwise, look for a U position with two matches.

- If the matching edges are neighbors, rotate the whole cube so they are at the **back and right**.
- If the matches are opposite, do the sequence once from any front side. Then turn U to find neighboring matches and hold them at the back and right.

## Swap the remaining edges

Do the complete sequence, including its final U turn. Check the side colors again. If needed, find the two matches, hold them at the back and right, and repeat.

## Check before continuing

Yellow still forms a cross, every top edge matches its side center, and both lower layers are complete. Yellow corners can face any direction.
