## Your goal

Solve the four middle-layer edges while keeping the white layer complete.

## Choose an edge

Find a top-layer edge with **no yellow sticker**. Turn U until its front-facing color matches the front center. Its top sticker tells you whether it belongs to the left or right. Rotate the whole cube before aligning if you need a different front color.

After each insertion, find another edge without yellow and line it up again. Keep white underneath throughout each sequence.

## If the edge is already in the middle

A wrong or flipped middle edge needs to come out first. Hold its slot at the front-right and insert a top-layer edge there using the right sequence. This ejects the trapped edge. Now line it up correctly on top and insert it into its own slot.

## Check before continuing

White is complete and the bottom two rows of every side match their centers. The top layer can still be mixed up.
