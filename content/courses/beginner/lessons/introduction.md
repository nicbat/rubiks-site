## Your goal

Solve a normal 3×3 cube, one layer at a time. You can pause, repeat examples, and return to any lesson. Marking a lesson complete records that you reviewed it; it isn't a test.

## Meet the pieces

- A **center** has one sticker and tells you the color of that side.
- An **edge** has two stickers. It belongs between the two matching centers.
- A **corner** has three stickers. All three colors must match its surrounding centers.

A finished face alone isn't a finished layer: the stickers around its sides must match too.

## Hold your cube

Our examples use **yellow on top, white underneath, and green at the front**. On this cube orange is right, red is left, and blue is back. Your physical cube may have a different color arrangement; use its centers as your guide.

## Read the moves

`R`, `L`, `U`, `D`, `F`, and `B` mean right, left, top, bottom, front, and back. A letter turns that face clockwise **as if you were looking straight at that face**. A prime, as in `R'`, means counterclockwise. `U2` is a half turn.

Keep the whole cube still while doing a sequence. Turning the top face is different from rotating the entire cube.

## Try it

Play the example, then try `R` and `R'` on your cube. They undo each other. The cube viewer lets you step through moves, reset, and drag to look underneath.

For more move demonstrations, open [Notation](/guides/notation).

This course uses a layer-by-layer approach. Further reading: [Ruwix beginner method](https://ruwix.com/the-rubiks-cube/how-to-solve-the-rubiks-cube-beginners-method/).
