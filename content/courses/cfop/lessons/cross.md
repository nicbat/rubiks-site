## Plan the cross. See what comes next.

Four ideas for fewer moves and a smoother first pair.
:::

:::fact

### Check another colour

Compare white and yellow. Try more colours as you get comfortable. Choose a cross you can plan, with an easy first pair when possible.

Every cross has an **8-move-or-fewer** solution. Half turns count as one. [The research](https://www.cubezone.be/crossstudy.html)
:::

:::note
Hold white down, green front. Each picture shows the same cube from above and underneath. Coloured pieces are the ones to watch.
:::
